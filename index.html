Initiative for Dependable Agentic Systems

Agentic systems that safely and reliably automate complex scientific and operational work. DAS brings together systems, security, and AI-for-science researchers to build accountable agents for high-stakes workflows.

Research Copilot

Agentic systems designed for scientific research: Claude as a coworker, with provenance, local knowledge, and model-choice control built in.

Cowork with Claude on literature, data analysis, writing, and project memory.
  • Provenance / Audit: trace every action, source, and generated artifact.
  • Local Knowledge: paper wiki and project memory stay close to the researcher.
  • Local + Cloud Models: use Claude/GPT now and connect local models when needed.
  • Research Workflows: literature search, full-text reading, analysis, visualization, and writing.

Industry Collaboration

Start small, then deepen the channel

We use lightweight entry points to de-risk collaboration before moving into sponsored projects and long-term research partnerships.

01

TSC / Consulting

Contracted expert help through UD's Technology Service Center.

02

Pilot Research

A 3–6 month project around one workflow and one clear deliverable.

03

Gift / Project Funding

Support students and exploratory topics without over-scoping too early.

04

Strategic Partnership

Shared roadmap, repeat projects, student pipeline, and joint outputs.
