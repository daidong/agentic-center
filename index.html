Initiative for Dependable Agentic Systems

Agentic systems that safely and reliably automate complex scientific and operational work. DAS brings together systems, security, and AI-for-science researchers to build accountable agents for high-stakes workflows.

Build with a Partner

What can DAS build with a partner?

Agents for high-stakes workflows where reliability, security, provenance, and systems infrastructure matter — with explicit boundaries on data, tools, and human review.

01

Research Copilot

Literature, analysis, writing, and project memory with provenance.

02

Scientific Workflow Copilot

Plan, run, monitor, and debug computational research workflows.

03

Infrastructure Agent

Diagnose logs, tune systems, and explain operational decisions.

04

Secure Agent Sandbox

Test MCP tools, plugins, permissions, data-flow risks, and audit trails.

05

Campus Service Agent

Triage tickets, retrieve policies, route forms, and support staff workflows.

Example Product

Research Copilot

Agentic systems designed for scientific research, with provenance, local knowledge, and model-choice control built in.

Research workflows with provenance, local knowledge, and model-choice control.
  • Provenance / Audit: trace every action, source, and generated artifact.
  • Local Knowledge: paper wiki and project memory stay close to the researcher.
  • Local + Cloud Models: use cloud models now and connect local models when needed.
  • Research Workflows: literature search, full-text reading, analysis, visualization, and writing.
  • Upcoming: Security · Safety · Scientific workflows.

Industry Collaboration

Start small, then deepen the channel

We use lightweight entry points to de-risk collaboration before moving into sponsored projects and long-term research partnerships.

01

TSC / Consulting

Service contract through UD's Technology Service Center.

02

Pilot Research

A 3–6 month project around one workflow and one clear deliverable.

03

Gift / Project Funding

Support students and exploratory topics without over-scoping too early.

04

Strategic Partnership

Shared roadmap, repeat projects, student pipeline, and joint outputs.

Start small → de-risk a pilot → fund a project → build a durable research partnership. We define the workflow, data boundary, success criterion, and review points before building. UD Technology Service Center ↗
